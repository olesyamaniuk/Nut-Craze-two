Color sort puzzle

"Nut Craze: Color Sort Puzzle" is a highly addictive and fun sorting game that challenges players to organize nuts of different colors into designated tubes.

With engaging mechanics and a visually appealing design, the game offers a relaxing yet strategic experience that keeps players entertained for hours. Whether you are a casual player looking for a stress-free pastime or a puzzle enthusiast aiming for perfection, this game is perfect for all ages.

Key highlights of the game include hundreds of unique levels with increasing difficulty, smooth and intuitive drag-and-drop controls, and a satisfying sense of accomplishment when sorting the nuts correctly. With vibrant animations, dynamic sound effects, and an immersive gameplay loop, "Nut Craze" stands out as one of the most enjoyable puzzle games available today.
